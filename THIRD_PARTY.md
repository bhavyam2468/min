The fonts are the unmodified variable fonts from [google/fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts),
renamed. The icons are path data from [lucide-static](https://www.npmjs.com/package/lucide-static).

The token model (OKLCH roles, surface flip, gesture zones) follows
[bhavyam2468/token-studio](https://github.com/bhavyam2468/token-studio).
